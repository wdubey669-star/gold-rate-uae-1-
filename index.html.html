How Gold Prices Are Set in the UAE

Gold is traded worldwide in US dollars per troy ounce. Because the dirham is pegged to the dollar, the UAE price follows the international spot price closely. In Dubai, the Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group (DGJG) publishes official retail rates per gram for each karat several times a day, and jewellers across the city use them.

Prices move with global factors — interest rates, the US dollar, inflation, central-bank buying and geopolitical events — so the same ring can cost noticeably more or less from one week to the next.

Making Charges

Jewellers add a making charge for the craftsmanship, either per gram or as a percentage of the gold value. Simple chains may carry a low charge; intricate or branded designs can cost much more. Making charges are usually negotiable — and they're not returned when you sell the piece back.

VAT on Gold in the UAE

Jewellery

Gold jewellery is subject to 5% VAT, charged on the total price including making charges. Visitors may be able to reclaim part of the VAT through the UAE's Tax Refund for Tourists scheme — ask the retailer.

Investment gold

Gold of 99% purity or higher in a tradable form — such as bars and some coins — is generally treated as investment gold and zero-rated for VAT.

Hallmarks & Quality

Look for the karat stamp (e.g. 22K or 916) and a hallmark from an approved assay office. Reputable shops provide an itemised invoice showing weight, karat, gold rate, making charge and VAT — keep it, as you'll need it to sell or exchange the piece later.

Where to Buy Gold in the UAE

Gold Souk, Deira

Hundreds of shops in one place — ideal for comparing designs and negotiating making charges.

Jewellery chains

Branches in malls across every emirate, with fixed prices and buyback policies.

Banks & bullion dealers

For bars and coins; some banks also offer gold savings accounts.

Digital gold

Apps that let you buy fractional gold backed by physical bullion — check the provider is licensed.

Selling or Exchanging Gold

When you sell, shops pay based on the day's rate for the gold's purity and weight, usually minus a small deduction — making charges and VAT aren't refunded. Exchanging old gold for new jewellery often gets a better deal than selling for cash. The calculator above estimates a typical resale value.

Gold Buying Tips

Check the live rate before you go. Then compare it with the rate on the shop's invoice.

Negotiate making charges, not the gold rate. The gold rate is fixed by the market; the making charge isn't.

Weigh it yourself. Ask to see the piece weighed and keep the itemised invoice.

For investing, buy bars or coins. Lower premiums and no making charges compared with jewellery.

Gold Souk guide

The Gold Souk in Deira is home to hundreds of jewellers under one roof. Prices for gold itself follow the official daily rate, so the real bargaining happens on making charges.

  • Go in the eveningCooler, and most shops open late
  • Ask for an itemised invoiceWeight, karat, rate, making, VAT
  • Watch it weighedOn a calibrated digital scale
  • Compare 3 shopsMaking charges vary a lot

Spotting Real Gold

✓Look for

  • Karat or purity stamp — 24K/999, 22K/916, 21K/875, 18K/750.
  • Hallmark from an approved assay office.
  • Itemised invoice from a registered shop.
  • Consistent colour with no flaking at edges or clasps.

✕Be wary of

  • Prices well below today's gold rate.
  • Street sellers or social-media offers without a shop.
  • Magnetic pieces — pure gold isn't magnetic.
  • No invoice — you'll struggle to resell it later.

Caring for Gold Jewellery

01Store pieces separately

Soft gold scratches easily — keep each piece in its own pouch or lined box.

02Remove before swimming

Chlorine and harsh chemicals can damage gold and gemstones.

03Clean gently

Warm water, a drop of mild soap and a soft brush, then dry with a lint-free cloth.

04Check clasps yearly

A quick check by a jeweller prevents losing a chain or bracelet.

Exchanging Old Gold for New

01Bring the piece and invoice

The original invoice makes the process faster and builds trust.

02Weighing and testing

The jeweller weighs the gold and tests its purity in front of you.

03Valuation at today's rate

The old gold is valued at the day's rate for its karat, minus any deduction.

04Credit towards new jewellery

The value is deducted from your new purchase — you pay making charges and VAT on the new piece.

Buying Gold as a Tourist

Dubai is a favourite place to buy gold on holiday. Keep the itemised invoice, ask the retailer about the Tax Refund for Tourists scheme, and check your home country's customs allowance for gold before you fly. If you carry cash and valuables worth more than AED 60,000 when leaving the UAE, you must declare them.

Gold Glossary

Spot price

The current international price for immediate delivery, in USD per troy ounce.

Premium

The amount above spot charged for bars and coins.

Making charge

The jeweller's fee for crafting a piece.

Karat

Measure of purity out of 24 parts.

Fineness

Purity in parts per thousand, e.g. 916 for 22K.

Troy ounce

31.1035 grams — the standard unit for gold prices.

Tola

11.664 grams — widely used in the UAE and South Asia.

Assay

Testing gold to confirm its purity.
